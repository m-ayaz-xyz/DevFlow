Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

At DevFlow, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our platform handles information when you visit and use our developer job discovery platform.

1. Information We Collect

DevFlow does not currently require users to create an account to browse job listings. We do not intentionally collect personal information through job browsing.

If you use the Contact form, the details you enter are passed to your default email application. DevFlow does not currently submit or store those messages on its server.

2. Third-Party Job Data

DevFlow displays job opportunities using third-party sources, including the Remotive API. Job information, company details and descriptions may be provided by these external services.

When you click an Apply or Source link, you may be redirected to an external website. Those websites have their own privacy policies and terms.

3. Local Storage and Caching

DevFlow may use your browser's local storage to cache job listing data and improve loading performance. Cached job data is automatically refreshed according to the application's cache policy.

You can clear this stored data through your browser settings. Clearing it may remove cached information.

4. Cookies and Tracking

DevFlow does not currently require cookies for account authentication. If analytics, advertising or other tracking services are added in the future, this policy will be updated to explain their use.

5. Data Security

We aim to keep the platform secure. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

6. Third-Party Websites

DevFlow may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their content, privacy practices or security policies.

7. Children's Privacy

DevFlow is a general job discovery platform and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our platform or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact page.
